True Shooting % (TS%)

TS% = PTS ÷ (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)). Measures scoring efficiency across all shot types — field goals, threes, and free throws. Unlike FG%, it accounts for the extra value of three-pointers and the cost of getting to the line.

Effective Field Goal % (eFG%)

eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA. Adjusts field goal percentage to account for the fact that three-pointers are worth 50% more than two-pointers. A player shooting 40% from three is equivalent to shooting 60% from two.
