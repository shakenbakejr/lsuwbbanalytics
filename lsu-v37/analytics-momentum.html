Momentum tracker — scoring

The season is split into three phases. Each number is that player's points per game average during that stretch. The dot color tells you whether they went up (green), down (red), or held steady (gray) compared to the previous phase. The final column shows their overall trajectory across the full season.

Phase 1: Non-conference (Nov–Dec, games 1–16)
Phase 2: SEC first half (Jan–early Feb, games 17–24)
Phase 3: SEC second half + tournament (late Feb–Mar, games 25–35)
Green dot = scoring went up vs. previous phase
Red dot = scoring went down vs. previous phase
Gray dot = within 1.0 ppg — held steady
