01 — Efficiency score

A single 0–100 composite score for every player that captures overall contribution beyond just points. Higher = more valuable to the team. Sorted from highest to lowest.

How it's calculated
Score = (PPG × 1.0) + (RPG × 0.7) + (APG × 0.8) + (SPG × 1.2) + (BPG × 1.0) − (TPG × 0.8) + FG% bonus
Weights reflect LSU's system priorities — steals are valued highest (1.2×) because Mulkey's pressure defense turns steals directly into points. Assists (0.8×) and rebounds (0.7×) reflect their supporting role. Turnovers are penalized (−0.8×). The FG% bonus rewards efficiency: every percentage point above 40% adds a small bonus. The raw result is normalized to a 0–100 scale based on realistic production ranges for this roster.
